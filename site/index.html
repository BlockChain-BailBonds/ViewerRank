RESEARCH CORE

From ranking score to causal intelligence.

The production path separates popularity, preference and quality, then measures the incremental information supplied by curator behavior.

01

Causal curator alpha

Correct exposure bias with inverse propensity and doubly robust estimators.

αᵤ = E[Qfuture − Qexpected | exposure]
02

Temporal curator state

Track changing topic expertise and discovery skill instead of freezing reputation.

zᵤ(t) = TemporalEncode(G<t, u)
03

Future quality forecasting

Predict multi-horizon quality at 1h, 24h, 7d, 30d and 90d without future leakage.

FVM(v,t,H) → Q̂v,t+H
04

Hidden-gem benchmark

Score whether the system surfaces eventual high-quality items while they are still obscure.

HiddenGemRecall@K
05

Calibration + uncertainty

Use calibrated probabilities and controlled uncertainty-driven exploration.

score = E[Q] + βσ
06

Curator information gain

Measure how much observing a curator reduces uncertainty about future value.

IG = H(Q) − H(Q | curator)
